The opportunity

What it is

A verified directory + automation engine that helps nonprofits discover, claim, and keep up with the free and discounted software they qualify for — CRM, design, email, websites, fundraising, security and more. Free to use; revenue comes from vendors, not nonprofits.

Go-to-market

Strategy

Positioning: the fastest, most trusted way for a nonprofit to discover and claim the free and discounted software it already qualifies for.

Target segments

Primary

Small & mid nonprofits

1–50 staff — lean teams, tight budgets, highest pain and fastest adoption.

Warm network

Ministries & churches

Faith-based orgs; strong word-of-mouth and referral effects.

Power users

Ops / IT / Development

The people who evaluate and adopt tools inside each org.

Channels, loops & monetization

Acquisition channels

  • SEO — each tool page is a landing page (JSON-LD, static pre-render)
  • Content — per-tool claim guides
  • Nonprofit communities & forums
  • Partnerships & associations
  • LinkedIn / social
  • Referrals & word-of-mouth

Growth loops

  • Weekly digest → retention
  • Wishlist → engagement + content ideas
  • Submit-a-tool → catalog growth
  • Reviews → trust + fresh SEO content
  • SEO flywheel → more tools = more pages

Monetization

  • Affiliate links (tracking built in)
  • Nonprofit-discount referral fees
  • Sponsored Deal-of-the-Week
  • Future: premium org features / API

Campaign assets

Email nurture sequence

A 5-email onboarding series for new signups, sending from your verified domain. Full copy with A/B subject lines in freefornonprofits-email-nurture.md.

1 · Welcome — "You're in: here's the free software you qualify for"
Day 0 · on signup confirm
Warm welcome + 3 quick wins (browse by need, save tools, set categories). CTA: Browse the directory.
2 · The biggest win — "$10,000/month in free ads (most nonprofits miss this)"
Day 2
Spotlight Google Ad Grants as the highest-ROI program. CTA: See Google Ad Grants.
3 · Build your stack — "Your nonprofit tech stack, for $0"
Day 4
A starter stack: Google Workspace, a free CRM, Canva, Mailchimp/Brevo, a website. CTA: Build your stack.
4 · Featured + community — "This week's Deal of the Week is live"
Day 7
Deal of the Week + the community wishlist (request & upvote tools). CTA: See this week's deal.
5 · Re-engage — "Still hunting for the right tools?"
Day 12 · only if no activity
Invite them to take the 2-minute survey to personalize recommendations. CTA: Personalize my recommendations.
